Franchise Legends lineup planner + live power rankings.

After login, paste the Fantrax league ID and pick your team. The app opens on League (power rankings / playoff picture / division standings) pulled live from Fantrax. My Lineup is the contract diamond planner.

Rankings: 50% last-2-weeks PF, 30% season weekly PF average, 20% win%. Weekly PA is the average of unique opponents that week (1–4 games, not hardcoded). Regular season only (periods 1–lastRegularSeasonPeriod).
